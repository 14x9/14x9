<Section title="Balance the budget">
  The second chapter offers a choose-your-own-adventure experience, letting
  users test common budget solutions. Partisan discourse obscures the costs of
  fixing the deficit, so we highlight inevitable trade-offs, showing no
  perfect solution exists. These trade-offs are both challenging and
  revealing.
</Section>

<Section title="A budget calculator for the pros">
  The final chapter is a budget calculator for economic bloggers and policy
  staffers, offering an industry-standard tool to evaluate proposals and guide
  decisions. With real-time data, users can test policies, cut costs, and
  boost the economy to showcase their strategy.
</Section>
