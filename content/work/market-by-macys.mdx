  Macy's introduced Market by Macy's, a vibrant retail concept centered on
  curation, discovery, and community engagement. This dynamic brand captures the
  joy of exploration through a unique store experience and a website design that
  draws inspiration from the nostalgic charm of vintage newspaper ads showcasing
  “great finds.” Reflecting Macy's 150-year legacy, the website enhances the
  shopping journey with bold visuals and an intuitive interface, inviting users
  to connect with the brand's fresh, community-driven identity.
